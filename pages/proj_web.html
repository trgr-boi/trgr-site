# https://web.trgrboi.be

This is my site to advertise my web development "side hustle" (don't know what else to call it).
The design is kind of commercial, or at least more than I usualy do, but still wanted it to be something that I liked. It is inspired by swis web design but still feels like *'normal'* sites.

I do find it a bit hard to make it not look like a AI site, because most of the commercial sites use all of the typical stuff that AI designs without having an identity of it's own. I hope I succeeded at that. There are some demo sites on there to show what I could make for a client, but they are really commercial and very basic. If you ask AI to make it, it would probably look alike. But I swear I made it myself (AI has been used as a tool ofcourse, but the design is by me!).

I will add some things before I really start to market this. Things like price estimations, different types of demos,... BUt I am already happy with the result!
